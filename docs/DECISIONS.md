## Measurement and processing

| # | Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | The "plane model" is implemented as the per-row parameters (a, b, u0[, k]) constrained to degree-4 polynomials across rows. It is chosen over independent rows by the same ≥ 20 % / ≥ 1 µm leave-one-out rule. | 1/cos Δ across the fan is not linear, so a true plane leaves 0.06 mm errors at the 500 mm range. Degree 4 is exact to about 1 µm. A full camera + laser-plane model is a later option. | `optics.calibrate_triangulation(surface_deg=4)` |
| D2 | The model family (hyperbolic → distortion → cubic) is chosen once for the whole sensor, from LOO RMS pooled over all rows. It is not chosen per row. | Per-row choice lets noise decide. Pooling is more robust. | `optics.calibrate_triangulation` |
| D3 | The calibrated quantity is z = d_rail / cos φ̂_k. | z·cos φ then reproduces the rail distance exactly, so errors in the row elevations φ do not reach the radius. The rail zero offset is absorbed by R0 on the ring. | `optics`, `sim/rig.py` |
| D4 | The local cylinder fit per band is **shape-aware**. The tilt is solved jointly with the order-8 Fourier profile (variable projection). The plain circular cylinder fit is kept as the start value and as the mode `per_band_circular`. | When the piston is fast there are only about 1.7 sweeps per band, so head angle and strip height are correlated. A circular fit then lets ovality leak into the tilt: up to 0.048 mm D error in simulation. The shape-aware fit gives 0.011 mm, and the global-tilt mode gives 0.015 mm. A per-band tilt also absorbs slow lateral drift of the piston. | `fitting.refine_axis_with_shape`, `ProcessingConfig.tilt_mode` |
| D5 | The Fourier fit has an extra linear taper term g·(s − s_mid). Diameters are given at band mid-height. | Inside a 30 mm band near the top the wall can change by more than 0.1 mm. Without the term, uneven sampling in depth biases the result. | `ProcessingConfig.fourier_taper` |
| D6 | Bands where D changes by more than 0.10 mm across the band are flagged "steep axial gradient". At the ring-reversal ridge the band value depends on the exact height (±2 mm gives up to 0.3 mm). | This is physics, not instrument error. The report says so, and the validation lists these bands separately. | `ProcessingConfig.gradient_flag_mm` |
| D7 | Profile bands are 30 mm high at a 30 mm step. The standard positions use extra bands centred on the depths in `EngineSpec.measuring_depths`: 16 depths, denser near the top. | Gives a VAK-like table plus a full profile. | `ProcessingConfig`, `EngineSpec` |
| D8 | Outliers: residual from the band's Fourier fit beyond max(5·1.4826·MAD, 0.1 mm), iterated up to 5 times. They are counted per band, drawn on the wall map and listed in the CSV. They are never dropped silently. | As in the brief. Real deep local damage (≥ 0.1 mm, narrow) is also marked. It shows on the map as a red cluster, which is intended. | `pipeline.analyze_band` |
| D9 | The ellipse cross-check flags a band when the ellipse and Fourier D max/D min differ by more than 0.05 mm. | Detects lobed (clover-leaf) or locally worn sections. | `ProcessingConfig.ellipse_flag_mm` |
| D10 | Angles in the liner frame are measured from FORE, counter-clockwise seen from above: PORT 90°, AFT 180°, STBD 270°. D_FA = D(0°), D_PS = D(90°). | Any fixed convention works. This one is stated on every report. **R** — UMA may prefer clockwise, or exhaust/manoeuvring side. | `pipeline`, `report` |
| D11 | Yaw (FORE direction) comes from the operator's placement estimate. It is refined from the scavenge-port pattern when the ports are in view: the wall-hit fraction is used against azimuth, n-th harmonic phase, resolved within ± half a port pitch of the estimate. | A 3° yaw error moves D_FA/D_PS by up to ovality × 0.05. The ports are always seen at the start of the stroke. The port phase relative to FORE must be entered per engine type. | `pipeline.estimate_yaw_from_ports`, `EngineSpec.port_phase_deg` |
| D12 | Height reference: crown depth = A + B·(upward **laser distance module** reading). A and B come from two anchors: BDC at the start, and the stop. For the stop, the liner-top edge is used where the strip reaches it (highest row that still sees wall, averaged over all head angles); otherwise the stop crank angle read at the flywheel. Smoothing is local-linear, ±1.5 s. The multizone ToF (central zones, 2nd-smallest) is the fallback. | No cable, no crank encoder. The device only moves vertically, so a narrow beam hits the same spot on the cover all stroke and stays linear. Multizone ToF footprints grow with range and slide from the valve face onto the cover dome: up to 15 mm error on the 450 bore in simulation. With the laser module the simulated height error is ≤ 1.2 mm (980 bore, top-edge anchor) and ≤ 2.7 mm (450 bore, flywheel anchor, ±0.25° reading). | `pipeline.height_model`, `pipeline.top_edge_anchor`, `sim/world.RangeFinderTruth` |
| D13 | Thermal compensation: global Δu0(T), κ(T), Δb(T) (quadratic in T − T_cal), calibrated in a climatic chamber at 20/40/60/80 °C. It is applied per frame with the camera temperature sensor. Before arming, the device soaks until dT/dt < 0.3 K/min (max 15 min). | Uncompensated drift is about 1 mm on z at 80 °C (see VALIDATION). The optical bench is Invar. | `optics.ThermalModel`, `ControllerConfig.soak_*` |
| D14 | Systematic standard uncertainty u_sys = 0.008 mm, combined with the per-band Fourier covariance to give U(k=2). | Taken from the RMS errors of the simulation campaign. It must be replaced by real ring/CMM comparisons. | `example.U_SYS`, `cli --u-sys` |

## Device and operation

| # | Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Optical presets are three interchangeable, factory-calibrated optical modules (camera + lens + 405 nm line laser on an Invar bench) on a kinematic mount: **S** 400–560 (f 6 mm, B 50 mm), **M** 560–760 (f 8, B 60), **L** 760–980 (f 12, B 65). Sensor: 1280×800, 3 µm global shutter. | §3 of the brief was missing. Fixed-focus glued modules avoid refocus repeatability problems. σ_z at the far end of each range is 0.076 / 0.088 / 0.090 mm per point. | `config.PRESETS` |
| E2 | The laser fan runs from −0.5° to the preset top (14/12/9°). The lowest ray clears the 8 mm arms by more than 2 mm at the 175 mm reach. | "Strip starts at crown level", no blind zones (unit-tested). | `config.PRESETS`, `geometry.arm_clearance` |
| E3 | Sweep ±190° with a trapezoid profile (accel 2000°/s²). Rate = 380° / (0.75 × time a wall point stays in the strip at peak piston speed). This gives about 107–130°/s at 200 fps, 0.54–0.65° per frame. The flex cable takes ±190°, so no slip ring is needed. | Every height is swept over a full turn while it is in view. | `kinematics.plan_scan` |
| E4 | Turning gear 0.1 rpm (default; settable). One stroke takes about 4.5 min. | Typical turning-gear speed. **Confirm on UMA's engines**: plan_scan resizes everything. | `plan_scan(turning_gear_rpm)` |
| E5 | Planned stop leaves 60 mm between the device top and the lowest overhead feature. ALARM = 1.5 × piston speed at the stop × (4 s reaction + 0.5 s stop), at least 10 mm. WARN = margin − 10 mm, debounced 0.5 s. ALARM also fires on time-to-contact < 8 s. | Simulation: an operator who misses the stop and reacts to the buzzer in 4 s keeps more than 5 mm (test). The radio is not involved. | `kinematics.plan_scan`, `device/safety.py` |
| E6 | ToF clearance = second-smallest vertical distance among the zones that look at the space above the device. Zones that see the liner wall are ignored. Loss of both sensors while armed gives FAULT, which sounds like an alarm (fail-safe). After 30 s of FAULT the recording closes. | A wall at about 1.4 m would otherwise mask the roof at about 2.6 m. | `device/safety.py`, `ControllerConfig.fault_end_s` |
| E7 | The laser is enabled only when both ToF sensors see a roof closer than 4 m (device inside a cylinder) and the soak is complete. | Eye safety for a class 3R/3B line laser. | `device/controller.py` |
| E9 | Added an upward narrow-beam laser distance module (±1 mm class, about 45 USD) for the height reference. The two VL53L8CX are kept for crush safety only. | See D12. It does not change the fixed requirements: safety still rests on the planned stop angle and the two ToF sensors. | `device/drivers.RangeFinder`, BOM |
| E8 | BLE carries state changes, 1 Hz status, safety events and a quick-look diameter every 5 s (Kåsa on a decimated slice, mid row). Packet loss is tolerated. | Status and results to the base station; full processing after retrieval. | `device/controller.py` |

## Simulation assumptions (to replace with real data)

Engine geometry is generic. The 980-bore example uses crown 15 mm below the liner top at TDC, 12 mm overhead clearance at TDC, 24 ports of 11°, and ports from 2400 to 2700 mm. Wear, ovality, clover-leaf, scratches and port layout are invented but plausible. Camera, encoder, ToF and thermal coefficients are typical datasheet-class values. **None of this comes from an engine maker or from UMA's measurements.**

## Brief discrepancy

"A chord through an axis 20 mm off centre is about 0.8 mm short on a 500 bore." The full chord perpendicular to the offset is e²/R = **1.6 mm** short. **0.8 mm** is the shortfall of each half-chord, i.e. a radius read through the device axis. The conclusion (always use the fitted centre) is unchanged. See `tests/test_optics_geometry.py::test_chord_through_offset_axis_is_short`.
